## Series Finale

A confession to his wife:

> "I didn't do it for the family. I liked it. I was good at it. I hadn't felt that alive in a long time."

The camera pulls back: behind him — a burning Grafana dashboard, all red.

## Moral

He sold the craft for velocity — and got an empire held together by duct tape and hallucinations, with not a single line he can vouch for.
