## Season 1 — "Move Fast" (10 episodes) ✅

The season opens with a flash-forward — Bely on a data-center rooftop, in his boxers, a Grafana dashboard burning red behind him. All ten episodes are the road to how he got there.

| # | Title | About |
|---|---|---|
| S01E01 | [Works on My Machine](./episodes/s01e01-works-on-my-machine.md) | The lab moves into a van — onto a Hetzner box in the basement. Bely meets Jess and "cooks" for the first time. |
| S01E02 | [Deploy on Friday](./episodes/s01e02-deploy-on-friday.md) | Bely deploys code he didn't read. An `auth bypass` ships to prod. |
| S01E03 | [Retry With Backoff](./episodes/s01e03-retry-with-backoff.md) | Mike's debut: he puts out the incident in silence and charges hourly, up front. |
| S01E04 | [Code Review](./episodes/s01e04-code-review.md) | The meeting with Gus at The HUB. Saul's debut. "It's a corpse with good posture." |
| S01E05 | [Standup](./episodes/s01e05-standup.md) | The double life. Sveta launders the tech debt. Hank traces a repo from 2008. |
| S01E06 | [Merge Conflict](./episodes/s01e06-merge-conflict.md) | War with Emil and Craze. Gus shuts down a competitor with one Slack message. |
| S01E07 | [Chekhov's console.log](./episodes/s01e07-chekhovs-console-log.md) | A forgotten logger leaks all the keys. Bely crosses the line: he weaponizes the leak. |
| S01E08 | [Rollback](./episodes/s01e08-rollback.md) | The empire is taken hostage. Hank finds the napkin from 2008. "Pass the salt, Val." |
| S01E09 | [Force Push](./episodes/s01e09-force-push.md) | A supply-chain trap. Gus falls — and a cascade turns Grafana red across the city. |
| S01E10 | [Postmortem](./episodes/s01e10-postmortem.md) | **Finale.** The pilot's rooftop pays off. "Say my namespace." The confession. |

## Series Finale

The camera pulls back: behind him — a burning Grafana dashboard, all red. Bely is not legacy anymore.

## Moral

He sold the craft for velocity — and got an empire held together by duct tape and hallucinations, with not a single line he can vouch for.
