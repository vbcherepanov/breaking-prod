## Leads

### Valentin Bely / `Heisenbug`
Old-school senior, 52. For 25 years he wrote C++ and Java by hand and knew every byte of his code. He *understood* — until he got a taste of speed. Underground, he's known as **Heisenbug**: the bug that disappears the moment you look at it. His code is recognized by its handwriting — it all compiles, it's all green, perfectly formatted, and nobody on earth knows how it works. Including the author.

> — Say my namespace.
> — …Heisenbug.
> — You're goddamn right.

### Jess
Gen-Z junior, 24. He's the one who dragged Bely into this. "Yo chief, I built three startups over the weekend." Can't read a stack trace, but feels the vibe like no one else. The face of the operation. A former intern of Bely's, whom he once kicked out for submitting someone else's code.

## Family

### Sveta (Skyler)
Bely's wife. A PM running three projects at once from her phone. At first horrified by what's happening, she later starts laundering the technical debt: hiding it across microservices, reframing it as a "deliberate architectural trade-off."

### Hank
Bely's brother-in-law, 48, principal security engineer. Loud, Hawaiian shirt, can of energy drink. He's hunting the mysterious `Heisenbug`, whose PRs blow up prod all over town. He has no idea he has dinner with him every Sunday.

## Business

### Gus Fring
Owner of a chain of no-code SaaS incubators — that's the cover. In reality, the industry's largest distributor of slop. Calm, polite, terrifying. Shuts down prod with a single Slack message.

### Saul Goodman
An AI consultant with a gradient-heavy landing page. Spin-off: **"Better Call GPT."** "Prod down? Don't panic — I've got a prompt."

### Mike
A grey-haired SRE. Comes out at night, silently puts out incidents, asks no questions. Charges hourly. Up front.
